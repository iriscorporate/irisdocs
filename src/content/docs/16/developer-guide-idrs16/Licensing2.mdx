### Licensing and protection types

The software licensing depends on the protection modes and the customer’s demands.

Under special circumstances, I.R.I.S. can provide partner integrators with custom licenses. Usually, this type of protection is given by I.R.I.S. under a very specific license agreement for large deployment projects and on a business level that represents substantial yearly revenue for I.R.I.S.
