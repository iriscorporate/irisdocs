## Rules of Technical Support

If your **Maintenance contract** is in order, you are entitled to **Technical Support**.

Support is provided by our R&D department, directly.

The **rules** are slightly different between ***LTS* (Long-term support)** and ***NON-LTS versions* (standard)**:

| VERSION | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **LTS** (Long-Term Support) | Support and Updates are provided *for 5 years* from the *first* date of release. |
| **NON-LTS** (standard) | Support is provided for 1 year from the date of release.  Updates are provided on the next version OR on the nearest active LTS version. |

:::note
Usually, we favor fixing bugs on the most recent LTS release, which will be more useful to you thanks to improvements, security fixes and additional bug fixes.
:::
